# Image credits

The recipes in the online catalog were written by an AI model and have no
photos. So that the app does not look empty, it shows one of twelve food photos
instead, picked from words in the recipe title
(`constants/illustrativePhotos.ts`). They only illustrate the kind of dish, and
recipe pages label them **Illustrative photo**.

The photos are part of the app only. The recipe payloads, the fixtures and
every byte that was measured are unchanged: a generated recipe's `image` field
is still its `generated://` reference.

All twelve are from [Unsplash](https://unsplash.com) under the
[Unsplash License](https://unsplash.com/license), which allows free use,
including in apps, without asking for permission. Credit is not required, but
it is given here. Each licence was checked on the photo's own page on
22 October 2026. The files were made with Unsplash's own image service: cropped
to 640 × 400, WebP, each under 60 KB.
